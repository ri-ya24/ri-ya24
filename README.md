## 🌸 About Me

<!-- 🎬 SIDE GIF — Baad me add karenge -->

I don't just look at data — I look for the **story behind it**.

What excites me about analytics is the **"why"** — Why did something happen? What pattern is hiding in the noise? How can data help make better decisions?

Currently pursuing my **BCA at the University of Lucknow**, I'm building practical skills in **SQL, Python, Excel, Power BI, and Tableau** — focusing not just on learning tools, but on developing an **analytical mindset** to solve real-world problems.

I believe in **curiosity over comfort**, **consistency over shortcuts**, and **learning through doing** — even when progress feels slow.

I'm also exploring where **Data Analytics** meets **Business Strategy, Finance, and Generative AI** — because the best analysts don't just understand data, they understand the **business behind it**.

### 📍 Quick Info

- 🎓 Currently pursuing **BCA**
- 🔹 Building skills in **SQL, Python, Excel, Power BI & Tableau**
- 🔹 Exploring **Business Strategy, Finance & Decision-Making**
- 🔹 Interested in **Generative AI & AI-powered analytics**
